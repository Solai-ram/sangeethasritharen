Our Story

From an unexpected match to a lifetime commitment — our journey has been filled with beautiful moments, genuine connections, and endless laughter. Join us as we celebrate the next chapter of our love story.

Tamil Matrimony Match

Our story began with a simple Tamil Matrimony match. Once the profile was accepted, our families connected effortlessly. Around the same time, one of us viewed the other's LinkedIn, leading to the first message — a small beginning that opened the door to something beautiful.

Our first chat on March 29th felt surprisingly natural. What started as a simple exchange soon grew into long, meaningful conversations on Instagram. Right from the beginning, we felt a comfort that didn't feel new at all.

We met for the first time on May 9th, a moment filled with ease, warmth, and genuine connection. That day, we both felt a sense of trust and belonging that made everything feel right.
